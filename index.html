The data

How this works

The city publishes no streetlight inventory, so lamp positions come from the 311 complaints filed about them. Every “light out” report on file marks a lamp that exists. Reports are deduplicated into roughly 55 m cells, and each cell is joined to its neighbours along the street to draw the glow.

Routes come from OSRM’s walking profile. Each is scored by the lamps within 80 m and the tree canopy within 40 m, normalised per kilometre.

Tree canopy is inferred the same way, from tree-trim requests. Streets that haven’t been trimmed for longer count as shadier.
