Tap two points for a brightly-lit route

Safe route

Tap inside Chicago to drop a start, then tap again for the destination. Each lamp emits a circular glow that overlaps with its neighbours, so a busy street reads as a continuous light strip and a quiet block dims between lamps. Trees (day mode) work the same way with dark shade discs. Toggle open / resolved 311 complaints to feed those lamp locations into the strip too.

Sources: 311 datasets zuxi-7xem, 3aav-uy2v, v6vf-nfxy (proxies for lamp positions). Tree trims snkf-esnw (base CSV tree-current.csv) preprocessed into a leaf-weighted shade grid and a clustered display sample. Boundary qqq8-j68g. Buildings from OSM Overpass, pre-fetched into local tiles in data/buildings/.

Tree icons carry 1–7 leaf clusters; more leaves = more shade. Tier 4 (full canopy) means >3 years since last trim or an open trim SR. Routes are scored both by lamps within 80 m and leaf-weighted trees within 40 m of the path.

Live 311 reports fetched directly in the browser on load: v6vf-nfxy with created_date > '2026-05-23T00:00:00' for lamps, snkf-esnw with creation_date > '2026-05-27T00:00:00' for tree trims (Chicago local time). Refresh page to re-pull.
