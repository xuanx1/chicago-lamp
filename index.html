The data

How this works

The city publishes no streetlight inventory, so lamp positions come from the 311 complaints filed about them. Every “light out” report on file marks a lamp that exists. Reports are deduplicated and counted per ~55 m cell. Each cell gets that many lamp posts, spaced along its streets on alternating kerbs.

Routes come from OSRM’s walking profile. Each is scored by the lamps within 80 m and the tree canopy within 40 m, normalised per kilometre.

Day and night follow the sun over Chicago at your device’s current time. The people and cars are simulated. They aren’t data.
